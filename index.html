Amounts use exact integer minor units. Implicit FX, self-payments, unknown references and excess precision are rejected. A batch either settles all listed whole payments simultaneously or changes no balances.

What this demonstrates
The default example has three accounts holding HKD 10 each and three cyclic payments of HKD 15. No gross payment can move first. An atomic net batch settles the cycle with unchanged balances. Netting and gross settlement have different capabilities; this example explains that difference.
The explorer is an exact search for this small model, bounded to 12 payments and at most 4,096 subsets. It starts from the declared initial balances, ignores the written execution sequence, and assumes every declared payment is available. The trace separately runs the program as written. A blocked gross attempt can be retried after a later payment provides liquidity.
Educational simulation using fictional accounts. No bank connection, real payment execution, FX, credit, collateral, fees, arrival times or production settlement guarantee. Currency checking and bounded search are established techniques; no claim of a globally novel algorithm is made.
